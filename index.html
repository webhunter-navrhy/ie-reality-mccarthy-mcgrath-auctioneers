Description

A well-presented 3-bedroom semi-detached home in a cul-de-sac in the popular College Manor development.

This property offers spacious living areas, a private rear garden, and driveway to the front. It is in an excellent location close to Cobh town centre and most schools of the town.

It also enjoys excellent transport links, with a regular bus service to and from Cork City stopping right nearby.

The property features double glazing throughout and natural gas central heating.

Perfect for first-time buyers, downsizers, or investors, it combines comfort, practicality, and convenience.
